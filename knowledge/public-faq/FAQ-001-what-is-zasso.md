# What is Zasso?

## Short Answer

Zasso is a Swiss-based technology company specializing in electric weed-control solutions for agriculture, municipalities, service providers, and other professional vegetation-management markets. Its core technology, Electroherb, uses controlled high-voltage electricity to treat unwanted plants without applying chemical herbicides. Zasso develops the power electronics, applicator concepts, safety architecture, and partner-ready systems that enable practical electric weeding in the field.

## Detailed Answer

Zasso designs and commercializes technologies for electric weeding: a non-chemical method that transfers electrical energy into target plants through direct contact with applicators or electrodes. The current passes through the plant and into the root zone/soil circuit during treatment, damaging plant tissues and disrupting water and nutrient transport. The aim is to provide a practical alternative or complement to herbicides and mechanical weed control, especially where customers want to reduce chemical residues, spray drift, soil disturbance, or herbicide-resistance pressure.

Zasso is best understood as a technology company and solutions enabler rather than only a machine manufacturer. Its work includes high-voltage power modules, control systems, applicators, product platforms, safety features, documentation, training concepts, and partnerships with equipment manufacturers or distributors. Public Zasso materials describe the company as designing and commercializing electric-weeding technologies for agriculture and municipalities, with Electroherb as the patented electric weed-control approach.

## What This Means for Customers

For customers, Zasso represents a route to professional, non-chemical weed management using specialized equipment and trained operation. The technology can be relevant for farms, vineyards, orchards, row-crop systems, municipalities, rail or infrastructure areas, and service contractors, depending on the local product configuration and application conditions.

The practical value is not that Zasso removes every need for other weed-control tools. It is that Zasso can help customers add a physical, residue-free mode of action to an integrated weed-management program. This can support reduction of herbicide use, management of herbicide-resistant weeds, lower spray-drift concerns, and reduced soil disturbance compared with some mechanical approaches.

## Evidence and Context

Zasso's public website states that the company designs and commercializes technologies supporting electric weeding in agriculture and municipality segments, and that its patented electric weed-control solution is a non-chemical alternative to herbicides: https://zasso.com/

Zasso's public technology page explains the working principle: high-voltage electricity is generated locally, passes through the applicator into the plant and soil circuit, and acts at the time of application without leaving chemical residues in the soil: https://zasso.com/technology/

Internal Zasso sales and technology materials reviewed for this FAQ describe Zasso as a Swiss-based specialist in tools and solutions enabling electric-weeding applications in agricultural and urban market segments. Those materials also support the positioning that Zasso works through product platforms, partners, training, safety systems, and service models, not only through standalone machinery.

External research also recognizes electric weed control as a commercially released weed-management tactic and identifies Zasso XPower as one example of commercially available electric weed-control technology. A 2025 Weed Science article notes both advantages such as no chemical residues and no spray-drift restrictions, and limitations such as slow application speed and high fuel use in some conditions: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/weed-science/article/electric-weed-controlhow-does-it-compare-to-conventional-weed-control-methods/488C45B191B97E84E34257E80EB9CFC6

UC Davis has also described university research with a Zasso electrical weed-control device as an alternative being studied for organic-farming weed management: https://www.plantsciences.ucdavis.edu/news/weed-day-2023

## Safe Sales Wording

Zasso is a Swiss electric-weeding technology company helping customers control unwanted vegetation with a physical, non-chemical mode of action.

Our Electroherb technology is designed to reduce reliance on herbicides by applying controlled electrical energy directly to target plants, with results depending on the crop, weed stage, soil conditions, machine setup, and operator training.

Zasso is not just selling hardware; it brings power electronics, applicator design, safety systems, training, and partner integration into a practical weed-management solution.

## Caveats

Zasso technology should not be described as universally replacing herbicides or mechanical tools in all conditions. Efficacy and economics depend on the weed species, growth stage, density, soil and moisture conditions, available product configuration, operating speed, energy input, safety procedures, and local regulations.

Avoid claims such as completely safe, zero environmental impact, always cheaper, or guaranteed control. It is safer to say that Zasso provides a non-chemical, residue-free mode of action that can reduce reliance on herbicides in suitable applications.
