# What is Electroherb?

## Short Answer

Electroherb is Zasso's branded electrical weed-control technology. It uses controlled high-voltage electrical energy, delivered through applicators or electrodes, to treat unwanted vegetation through a physical mode of action rather than a chemical herbicide. The technology is designed to damage plant tissue from the above-ground parts down toward the root system, with results depending on the target plant, field conditions, machine setup, applicator contact, speed, and trained operation.

## Detailed Answer

Electroherb is the name Zasso uses for its electrophysical weed-control platform. In practical terms, it is an electric weeding system: a power and control unit generates high-voltage electricity, applicators contact target vegetation, and the plant becomes part of a controlled electrical circuit. The electrical energy moves through the plant tissue and return path, creating cellular damage and heat effects that disrupt the plant's ability to transport water and recover.

The key difference from herbicides is that Electroherb does not rely on a molecular active ingredient. It is a physical treatment, so it does not leave chemical herbicide residues and it is not intended to create the same biochemical resistance pressure as repeated use of a single herbicide mode of action. The key difference from mowing or shallow mechanical weeding is that the goal is not simply to cut the plant above the soil surface, but to deliver enough energy through the contacted plant to reduce regrowth potential where conditions allow.

Zasso positions Electroherb as a professional technology platform, not just a single machine. Different product configurations can use the same basic principle for different use cases, such as agricultural, municipal, service-provider, or specialized vegetation-management applications. The value of the technology depends on matching the right equipment, applicator geometry, operating speed, energy delivery, crop/site context, and safety procedures to the customer's problem.

Because Electroherb uses high voltage, it must be treated as professional equipment. It requires appropriate machine design, operator training, safety distances, maintenance procedures, local compliance, and operating discipline. It should be presented as a strong non-chemical tool for integrated weed management, not as a universal replacement for every herbicide or mechanical method in every condition.

## What This Means for Customers

For customers, Electroherb offers a non-chemical, residue-free way to control unwanted vegetation where herbicide reduction, spray drift avoidance, resistance management, soil disturbance, public acceptance, or sustainability targets matter. It can be especially useful when customers need another mode of action alongside existing weed-control tools.

Customers should also understand that Electroherb is application-specific. Performance is affected by weed species, growth stage, plant moisture, root structure, soil conductivity, soil moisture, electrode contact, travel speed, dose, machine configuration, operator skill, and weather or fire-risk conditions. A good sales conversation should therefore connect Electroherb to the customer's specific crop, site, weed spectrum, operating window, and safety requirements.

## Safe Sales Wording

Electroherb is Zasso's electrical weed-control technology: it uses controlled high-voltage energy to treat unwanted plants without applying chemical herbicides.

It is designed to provide a physical mode of action that can support herbicide reduction, resistance management, and lower soil disturbance in suitable applications.

The best results come from matching the right Zasso configuration, operating conditions, safety procedures, and trained operator to the customer's crop or vegetation-management need.

## Caveats

Do not describe Electroherb as completely safe, universally effective, zero-impact, or guaranteed to replace herbicides in every situation. It uses high voltage and must be operated only with appropriate training, safety systems, maintenance, and local compliance.

Avoid claiming that Electroherb always kills roots, always prevents regrowth, always costs less, or works in all soils, crops, weeds, and weather conditions. Dense or mature vegetation, poor electrode contact, unsuitable speed, dry biomass, crop contact risk, soil and moisture conditions, and operator error can all affect performance or safety.
