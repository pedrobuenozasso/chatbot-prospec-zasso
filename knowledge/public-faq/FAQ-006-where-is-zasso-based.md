# Where is Zasso based?

## Short Answer

Zasso is headquartered in Zug, Switzerland. The company also has important operating locations in Brazil and Europe, including Zasso Brasil in Indaiatuba, Sao Paulo, and European operations connected to Germany and France. For customer conversations, it is safest to describe Zasso as a Swiss-based company with international operations.

## Detailed Answer

Zasso Group AG is based in Zug, Switzerland. Zasso's public website identifies the company as Swiss-based, and the public imprint lists Zasso Group AG at Grafenaustrasse 11, CH-6300 Zug, Switzerland. Public Zasso contact information also lists the headquarter address in Zug.

Zasso's operational footprint is broader than Switzerland. Public and internal materials consistently show that Zasso has an important Brazilian base in Indaiatuba, Sao Paulo, where Zasso Brasil operates. Zasso's public Brazil contact page lists the office at Rua Sergio Ueda 521, Park Comercial de Indaiatuba, Indaiatuba/SP, CEP 13347-442, Brazil. Internal Zasso Brasil legal and licensing materials also identify Rua Sergio Ueda, no. 521, Indaiatuba/SP as the registered and operating address for Zasso Brasil.

Customer-facing language should therefore distinguish the group headquarters from operating sites. The group is headquartered in Zug, Switzerland; Brazil is a major operational, engineering, manufacturing, R&D, and LATAM commercial base; and European operations are associated with Germany and France depending on the activity, product, and customer market.

## What This Means for Customers

Customers can present Zasso as an international electric-weeding technology company with Swiss headquarters and practical operational presence close to key agricultural and urban markets. This matters because customers, distributors, municipalities, growers, and partners may interact with different Zasso entities or teams depending on country, product, language, service model, and regulatory context.

For customers in Latin America, Zasso Brasil in Indaiatuba is an important local reference point. For European customers, Zasso's European operations and partners may be the more relevant contact route. For corporate, IP, group-level, or investor-facing matters, Zasso Group AG in Zug is the appropriate group-level reference.

## Safe Sales Wording

Zasso is headquartered in Zug, Switzerland, and operates internationally, with important teams and operations in Brazil and Europe.

For Latin America, Zasso Brasil in Indaiatuba, Sao Paulo, is a key operational base; for group-level matters, Zasso Group AG in Zug is the correct headquarters reference.

The simplest customer-facing wording is: Zasso is a Swiss-based electric-weeding technology company with international operations.

## Caveats

Do not imply that every Zasso office performs the same role. Headquarters, R&D, manufacturing, sales, service, certification, partner management, and local customer support may sit in different entities or locations.

Avoid giving legal, tax, contracting, or invoicing guidance from this FAQ. The correct contracting entity, address, and point of contact may depend on country, product, customer type, and commercial arrangement.
