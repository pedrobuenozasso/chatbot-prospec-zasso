# Is Zasso technology patented?

## Short Answer

Yes. Based on current Zasso materials, Zasso's electric weeding technology is protected by patents and patent families, alongside proprietary know-how, trademarks, product design, software, safety systems, and field experience. Customers should understand this as evidence that Zasso has invested in a defensible technology platform, not as a legal opinion about any specific patent claim, country, expiry date, or freedom-to-operate question.

## Detailed Answer

Zasso publicly describes its electric weed-control solution as patented. The company's website states that Zasso's electric weed-control solution is a non-chemical alternative to herbicides, and its technology page specifically refers to patented power modules used to modulate high voltage for controlled weeding results. Zasso's public company description also says the technology was originally developed in Brazil and targets both the shoots and roots of undesired plants using advanced lightweight high-voltage methods.

Internal Zasso materials support the same picture. Zasso's internal IP inventory identifies multiple patent families related to power module converter modularity, high-frequency power control, impedance matching, electrical-weeding safety controls, retractable applicators, fire-hazard mitigation, area desiccation, nematode control, multiple-power-peak PWM converter control, metal spark protection, and smart boots for handheld equipment. Older business materials also refer to a portfolio strategy with more than ten patents or patent applications, organized around core Zasso technologies such as power electronics, control modules, app/cloud systems, safety, platform features, applicators, mechanical components, crop-specific features, and certification-linked know-how.

For customers, the practical point is that Zasso is not simply selling a generic high-voltage implement. Its competitive position is based on a combination of formal IP, engineering know-how, safety design, applicator design, data/control systems, and accumulated field learning. In deep hardware, patents are only one layer of defensibility; repeatable product performance, service capability, certification work, training, and field experience also matter.

## What This Means for Customers

Customers can safely say that Zasso technology is patented and supported by a broader intellectual-property and know-how base. That can be relevant when evaluating supplier seriousness, long-term product development, technical differentiation, and partner confidence.

It should not be presented as a guarantee that every product feature is patented in every jurisdiction, that no third-party IP questions can ever arise, or that patents alone prove agronomic performance. For procurement, licensing, OEM partnerships, distribution agreements, or investor diligence, patent status should be confirmed with Zasso's current legal or IP documentation.

## Evidence and Context

Evidence reviewed for this answer included Zasso's GitHub FAQ/index state and internal technology overview; Zasso's internal Body of Knowledge and IP document; a SharePoint business presentation describing a patent portfolio strategy and more than ten patents or patent applications; recent Read.AI meeting summaries showing ongoing product, certification, commercial, and platform work; and public Zasso pages describing the solution as patented and referring to patented power modules.

Public technical context also supports caution. Electric weed control is an established category with historical patents and modern commercial machines from multiple companies. A 2024 Weed Science review notes that electric weed control has gained recent global interest and that further research is still needed on applicability, efficacy, and risks before broad adoption. That means Zasso's patent position should be framed as one strong part of the overall technology story, not as a substitute for application-specific evidence.

## Safe Sales Wording

"Yes. Zasso's Electroherb technology is protected by patents and supported by substantial proprietary know-how in power electronics, applicators, safety systems, and field application. For contract-level patent details by country or product, Zasso can provide the appropriate legal or technical documentation through the proper channel."

## Caveats

Patent coverage is jurisdiction-specific and can vary by patent family, product, feature, application, filing status, grant status, and expiry date. This FAQ is customer-facing commercial guidance, not legal advice, a patent landscape, a freedom-to-operate opinion, or a list of enforceable claims. Avoid giving customers unpublished patent numbers, internal claim strategies, confidential product details, or legal interpretations unless those materials have been approved for external use.
