# What crops or markets does Zasso focus on?

## Short Answer

Zasso focuses on markets where non-chemical weed control has clear practical value: agriculture, urban and landscaping, industrial areas, infrastructure, and service-provider operations. In agriculture, the most visible focus areas are specialty and permanent crops such as vineyards, orchards, citrus, coffee, berries and similar crops, with product development also pointing toward small crops, row crops and broader field applications where the fit is technically and economically suitable. The exact crop or market priority depends on region, product availability, certification, customer economics, support capacity and local demand.

## Detailed Answer

Zasso should be presented as an electric-weeding technology platform serving more than one crop or market. The company’s public and internal materials consistently group the opportunity into two main commercial segments: agriculture and urban/landscaping or infrastructure vegetation management.

In agriculture, Zasso’s strongest current customer-facing focus is in crops and systems where herbicide reduction, labor pressure, soil protection, organic or regenerative positioning, and repeated vegetation management are important. Public Zasso pages identify XPS for vineyards and orchards in Europe, E.CITRUS for citrus and similar wider-row fruit crops in Latin America, E.COFFEE for coffee, raspberry, blueberry and similar narrower-row crops in Latin America, and RAIDEN for paths, sidewalks, curbs and small agricultural plantations in the Americas. Internal product and investor materials also frame agriculture around specialty crops, permanent crops, nuts, olives, berry plantations, citrus, coffee, and future row-crop or in-row opportunities.

Outside agriculture, Zasso focuses on municipalities, landscaping contractors, facilities management, road and highway maintenance, cemeteries, sidewalks, parks, industrial areas, solar farms and similar non-crop environments. Public Zasso material describes XPU as a tractor-based solution for sidewalks, roadsides, cemeteries and large industrial areas, while internal product materials include urban, landscaping, maintenance, solar and infrastructure use cases.

The practical sales message is that Zasso does not need to claim that every crop is an immediate target. The best-fit markets are those where customers need a non-chemical, residue-free, low-soil-disturbance tool and where equipment speed, safety procedures, operator training, service model and cost per hectare or operating hour can make sense. Some applications are already represented by public products; others remain development, validation or market-expansion opportunities.

## What This Means for Customers

Customers should start by describing their application, not by asking whether Zasso works for every crop. The relevant questions are: What crop or site is being managed? What weeds are present? Is the goal inter-row control, under-vine or under-tree control, sidewalk or hard-surface maintenance, service-provider work, crop desiccation, or another application? What region, certification route, tractor or carrier vehicle, service support and training model apply?

For growers and land managers in vineyards, orchards, citrus, coffee, berries, small-crop systems, urban areas and industrial sites, Zasso may offer a relevant product or service route today, depending on the country. For broadacre row crops and highly specialized applications, the opportunity can be significant, but customers should treat availability and performance as application-specific and confirm the current approved configuration with Zasso.

## Safe Sales Wording

"Zasso focuses on applications where non-chemical weed control solves a real customer problem, especially specialty and permanent crops, urban and industrial vegetation management, infrastructure, and service-provider operations."

"In agriculture, Zasso’s visible focus includes vineyards, orchards, citrus, coffee, berries and small-crop systems, with additional opportunities in row crops and other applications where the technical and economic fit is validated."

"The right Zasso solution depends on the crop or site, country, certification status, carrier vehicle, support model and operating conditions, so availability should always be confirmed for the specific customer case."

## Caveats

Do not claim that Zasso is commercially available for every crop, every country or every use case. Public pages show clear regional limits for some products, and internal materials include development and expansion opportunities that should not be presented as fully commercial unless approved.

Avoid saying that Zasso is always cheaper, always more effective, or a universal replacement for herbicides. Crop fit, weed species, growth stage, soil conditions, operator training, safety procedures, product configuration, certification and local service coverage all affect adoption.
