# In which countries does Zasso operate?

## Short Answer

Zasso operates internationally, with public materials identifying offices or key operating locations in Switzerland, Brazil, Germany, and France. In practical customer terms, Brazil and Europe are core operating regions, while product availability, service coverage, demonstrations, and partner access can vary by country. The safest wording is that Zasso is a Swiss-based electric-weeding company with international operations and market activity through local entities, partners, and service channels where available.

## Detailed Answer

Zasso's group headquarters are in Zug, Switzerland. Public Zasso contact and imprint pages identify Zasso Group AG in Zug, and Zasso public materials also refer to offices or operating locations in Indaiatuba, Brazil; Aachen, Germany; and Paris, France. Zasso's About page describes Zasso GmbH in Aachen as the development and commercial base for Europe, and Zasso's Brazil contact page identifies Zasso Brasil in Indaiatuba, Sao Paulo.

For customer conversations, it is important to separate three ideas: legal or office presence, active commercial operations, and product availability. Zasso has a clear public footprint in Switzerland, Brazil, Germany, and France. It also has customer, partner, demonstration, or market-development activity in broader regions, especially Latin America and Europe, depending on the product and use case. Public Zasso releases and external coverage show activity in Brazil, Europe/Benelux, vineyards, urban weed control, and prior testing in the United States, but that does not mean every product is available for sale or service in every country.

A conservative answer is therefore: Zasso operates from Switzerland with important operating bases in Brazil and Europe, and serves customers through a mix of direct teams, local entities, partners, distributors, contractors, and service models where available. Customers should confirm country-specific availability, regulatory status, service coverage, training requirements, and commercial model before making a purchase or deployment decision.

## What This Means for Customers

Customers in Brazil and Latin America can generally treat Zasso Brasil as a key operational and commercial reference point. Customers in Europe may interact with Zasso's European team, Zasso GmbH in Germany, Zasso France or French commercial contacts, or local partners depending on the product, application, and country.

For markets beyond the core public office footprint, Zasso may still support trials, partnerships, research, licensing, or market-entry work, but customers should not assume full local sales, certification, spare-parts, training, or after-sales coverage until confirmed. This is especially important for safety-critical electric-weeding equipment, where local regulations, operator training, service capacity, and product certification can affect deployment.

## Safe Sales Wording

Zasso is headquartered in Switzerland and operates internationally, with important bases in Brazil and Europe.

Public Zasso materials identify offices or key operating locations in Zug, Switzerland; Indaiatuba, Brazil; Aachen, Germany; and Paris, France.

Country-specific availability depends on the product, application, partner coverage, certification status, service model, and local regulations, so we confirm the right route for each customer before deployment.

## Caveats

Do not say that Zasso has full sales and service coverage in every country. Office presence, partner activity, demonstrations, research projects, certification status, and full commercial availability are different things.

Avoid naming private customers, unannounced partners, internal sales targets, confidential contract details, pricing, financing, or certification timelines. For regulated, safety-critical, or public-sector deployments, country-specific compliance and operator-training requirements must be checked before making commitments.
