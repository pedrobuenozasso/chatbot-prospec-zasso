# How long has Zasso been developing electrical weeding technology?

## Short Answer

Zasso's modern group structure dates from 2016, but its electrical weeding lineage goes back much further. Public Zasso materials state that the technology was originally developed and commercialized in Brazil through Sayyou, founded in Botucatu in 1992, and that early electric weeding equipment was developed and field-tested in Brazil in the early 1990s. So it is fair to say Zasso builds on more than three decades of electric-weeding development experience, with the current Zasso Group platform developed internationally since 2016.

## Detailed Answer

There are two useful dates to separate. The first is the origin of the technology lineage: Zasso's public history says the company was born as Sayyou in Botucatu, Brazil, in 1992, where the Electroherb technology was originally developed and commercialized. The same public history credits early Brazilian electric-weeding work in the early 1990s as an important foundation for the Zasso name and technology story.

The second date is the modern Zasso Group structure. Zasso Group AG is headquartered in Zug, Switzerland, and public company materials state that the group and its European development and commercial base were established in 2016. Since then, Zasso has developed the technology into a broader international platform covering power electronics, applicators, product families, safety systems, training, field deployments, and partnerships.

This distinction matters because electrical weeding itself is not a brand-new scientific idea. External technical literature traces electrical weed-management patents back to the late 1800s, with significant research and early commercial machines in the 1970s and 1980s. Zasso's contribution is not simply discovering that electricity can kill plants; it is turning that principle into practical, modern electric-weeding systems for professional use.

## What This Means for Customers

Customers can position Zasso as an experienced specialist, not a recent experiment. The company combines a long Brazilian development heritage with a modern Swiss/German/Brazilian operating structure and current field experience.

That history also helps explain why Zasso talks about electrical weeding as a system rather than a single machine. Effective customer deployment depends on power electronics, electrode contact, crop and weed conditions, safety design, operator training, after-sales support, and local regulatory requirements. Zasso's experience is valuable because the category is technically demanding and field conditions vary.

## Safe Sales Wording

Zasso's modern group was founded in 2016, but the technology lineage behind Electroherb goes back to Brazilian development work that began in the early 1990s.

A safe way to say it is: Zasso builds on more than 30 years of electric-weeding development heritage, while the current international Zasso Group has been scaling and industrializing the technology since 2016.

This is not a new lab concept; it is a mature technology lineage being developed into practical, professional weed-management systems.

## Caveats

Do not say that Zasso invented all electrical weeding or that the scientific principle started with Zasso. Electrical weed-control concepts and patents are much older than the company. It is safer to say Zasso has a long development heritage in modern electric weeding and has commercialized its own patented Electroherb technology.

Do not overstate field performance based only on company age. Experience supports credibility, but results still depend on product configuration, weed species, growth stage, soil moisture, soil conductivity, operating speed, training, maintenance, and local safety and regulatory requirements.
