# What does Zasso do?

## Short Answer

Zasso develops and commercializes electric weed-control solutions for professional agriculture, urban, landscaping, and infrastructure markets. Its Electroherb technology applies controlled high-voltage electricity through target plants to provide a non-chemical, residue-free mode of action for vegetation management. Zasso combines power electronics, applicators, control systems, safety concepts, training, and partner channels so customers can use electric weeding as part of practical weed-management programs.

## Detailed Answer

Zasso helps customers control unwanted vegetation without applying chemical herbicides. The company's core work is electric weeding: specialized equipment brings electrodes or applicators into contact with weeds, delivers a controlled electrical dose, and uses the plant-soil circuit to damage the plant tissues, including below-ground structures where conditions allow. This gives customers a physical mode of action that can complement or, in suitable applications, reduce reliance on herbicides, mowing, tillage, flame, hot water, or other non-chemical methods.

Zasso's role is broader than simply building one machine. Current Zasso materials describe the company as a provider of electric-weeding solutions with expertise in high-voltage power electronics, mechanical applicator design, control software, safety systems, field validation, and commercial models adapted to different segments. Its product and partnership approach addresses both agriculture, such as vineyards, orchards, row crops, coffee, citrus, and specialty crops, and urban or landscaping settings, such as municipalities, service contractors, roadsides, parks, cemeteries, and industrial areas.

In practice, Zasso works to turn electric weeding from a technical principle into deployable equipment and services. That includes machine platforms, applicators, digital and telemetry concepts, operator training, maintenance support, distributor or OEM partnerships, and use-case-specific operating guidance. The exact customer offer can vary by country, product, partner, certification status, and business model.

## What This Means for Customers

For customers, Zasso offers another tool for integrated weed management. It can be especially relevant where herbicide use is restricted, residues or spray drift are a concern, resistant weeds are a problem, soil disturbance should be minimized, or customers want a non-chemical option for public or sensitive areas.

The main customer benefit is not a universal replacement promise. Zasso gives customers access to a professional electric-weeding approach that may reduce chemical dependence, support organic or regenerative strategies where applicable, and add a different physical mode of action to the weed-control toolbox. Results depend on the weed species, growth stage, density, soil moisture, application speed, equipment configuration, operator training, and local safety or regulatory requirements.

## Caveats

Do not claim that Zasso always replaces herbicides, always lowers cost, works in every crop or climate, or has zero risk. Efficacy, operating cost, safety procedures, and suitability depend on local conditions, target weeds, product configuration, trained operation, and regulatory or certification requirements.

Avoid repeating confidential commercial forecasts, customer names, internal pricing, certification timelines, or private operational details from internal materials. Public claims should stay at the level of technology purpose, general market segments, and externally supported benefits.
