# What are the main Zasso products?

## Short Answer

Zasso's main products are electric-weeding solutions built around the Electroherb and XPower technology platforms. Public Zasso materials list tractor-based and applicator products such as XPU for urban and industrial areas, XPS for vineyards and orchards, XPA hydraulic-arm applications, XPH handheld applications, and region-specific products such as THOR, E.COFFEE, E.CITRUS, RAIDEN, IZI, CityZAS, and the eWeeding Roller. The exact product offered to a customer depends on the country, crop or site, carrier vehicle, certification status, and available local sales or service channel.

## Detailed Answer

Zasso should be presented first as an electric-weeding technology company, not as a single-machine company. Its product portfolio is organized around the Electroherb mode of action and the XPower family of equipment, with different machines and applicators adapted to different use cases.

For Europe, Zasso's public service page groups the XPower line around XPS for vineyards and orchards, XPU for urban applications, XPA as a hydraulic-arm solution for XPU, and XPH as a handheld solution for XPU. Zasso's public products page also lists XPH, CityZAS, eWeeding Roller, XPU, XPS, and XPA for European or Benelux contexts. These are the safest externally visible names to use when discussing current public European portfolio examples.

For Latin America and the Americas, Zasso's public products page lists THOR for urban applications, E.COFFEE for coffee, E.CITRUS for fruitculture, RAIDEN for small crops, and IZI as a consumer handheld product. The RAIDEN page describes it as a compact solution for paths, sidewalks, curbs, and small agricultural plantations, assembled on a motor-tiller or micro-tractor platform and currently commercially available only in Latin America.

Older public Zasso news and partner material also mention XP300, XPR, and other XPower concepts or roadmap items. These can be useful context, but sales teams should avoid implying that every historical or concept product is currently available in every market. For customer discussions, the practical answer is to identify the customer's application first, then confirm the currently approved product and commercial route for that region.

## What This Means for Customers

Customers should not choose a Zasso product by product name alone. They should start with the job to be done: vineyard or orchard weed control, urban hard-surface maintenance, industrial areas, coffee or citrus, small-crop work, handheld or hard-to-access applications, or service-provider operations.

Once the application is clear, Zasso can match the customer to the right product family, power level, applicator, carrier vehicle, training requirement, safety procedure, and after-sales model. A product listed for Europe may not be the appropriate route in Latin America, and a Latin American product may not be available or certified in Europe. The best customer-facing message is therefore: Zasso has several electric-weeding products, and the right one is selected case by case.

## Safe Sales Wording

"Zasso offers a portfolio of electric-weeding solutions rather than a single machine. Depending on the market and application, the portfolio can include XPower solutions such as XPS, XPU, XPA and XPH, as well as region-specific products such as THOR, E.COFFEE, E.CITRUS, RAIDEN, IZI, CityZAS and eWeeding Roller."

"The right Zasso product depends on the customer's crop or site, country, carrier vehicle, safety requirements, certification status, and local support route, so we confirm the recommended configuration case by case."

## Caveats

Do not promise that every named product is available in every country. Availability, certification, configuration, support, training, spare parts, pricing, and delivery timing must be confirmed for the specific market and customer use case.

Avoid presenting older roadmap or concept names as currently commercial unless current approval is confirmed. Avoid disclosing confidential internal names, development timelines, customer pilots, sales targets, pricing, partner negotiations, or certification plans unless they have been approved for external use.
