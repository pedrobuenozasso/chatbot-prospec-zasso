# Is Zasso technology commercially available?

## Short Answer

Yes. Zasso technology is commercially available, but availability depends on the product, region, application, certification status, and local sales or service channel. Public Zasso materials list multiple products for Europe, Latin America, Benelux, and the Americas, and external weed-science literature identifies Zasso XPower as an example of commercially available electric weed-control technology. Customers should confirm the right model, delivery route, training, certification, and after-sales coverage for their country and use case before making purchase or deployment plans.

## Detailed Answer

Zasso is beyond the prototype-only stage. Its public product pages list electric-weeding solutions across agricultural, urban, industrial, and specialized-crop applications. Examples include XPH, CityZAS, eWeeding Roller, XPU, XPS, and XPA in Europe or Benelux; THOR, E.COFFEE, E.CITRUS, and IZI in Latin America; and RAIDEN for the Americas. The public product listing is a strong customer-facing signal that Zasso has commercial product families, not only research concepts.

Commercial availability should still be described carefully. Not every product is available in every country, and not every configuration is suitable for every crop, weed target, carrier vehicle, operator profile, or regulatory environment. Some products may be sold directly, some may be supplied through partners, distributors, contractors, service models, or OEM relationships, and some may require region-specific certification, training, commissioning, or support planning.

Public partner materials also support the commercial-availability claim. New Holland material describes XPower XPS as part of the XPower family of electric weeding solutions for vineyards, developed for wine and fruit growers. A 2025 Weed Science article describes XPower from Zasso as a commercially available electric weed-control technology with applicability in viticulture using the XPS applicator. Zasso public news also reports integration of XPower into CNH Industrial's AGXTEND portfolio in Latin America and earlier market milestones for Electroherb equipment.

Internal Zasso context is consistent with this public picture. Repository materials describe Zasso as commercializing Electroherb and XPower technology through product families, equipment, partners, rentals, licensing, training, and service models. Recent Read.AI meeting summaries show active commercial, demonstration, product-update, training, and post-sales work in Brazil, Europe, and prospective international opportunities. Those internal signals support availability, but confidential details such as customer names, pricing, delivery dates, sales targets, certification timelines, contracts, financing, and unit counts should not be repeated externally unless specifically approved.

## What This Means for Customers

Customers can treat Zasso as a commercial electric-weeding technology provider, not as a purely experimental project. The next step is to match the customer's country, crop or site type, weed-control objective, carrier vehicle, safety requirements, operating speed, training needs, and service expectations to the right product or partner channel.

For a vineyard, orchard, municipal contractor, infrastructure operator, coffee grower, citrus grower, or row-crop customer, the answer may be different. A product that is listed for Europe may not be the correct commercial route in Latin America, and a Latin American agriculture product may not be available or certified in Europe. Sales teams should therefore answer "yes, commercially available" while immediately qualifying the answer by region, product, and operating model.

## Safe Sales Wording

"Yes. Zasso technology is commercially available in selected products and markets, with availability depending on the region, application, certification status, and local partner or service setup. The right next step is to confirm the customer's country, crop or site, machine configuration, training needs, and after-sales coverage so Zasso can recommend the appropriate commercial route."

## Caveats

Do not say that every Zasso product is available everywhere. Do not promise immediate delivery, certification, service coverage, financing, warranty terms, or local spare-parts support without checking the current country and product status. Avoid quoting confidential customer names, private contracts, sales targets, pricing, unit counts, internal certification timelines, or partner negotiations.

Commercial availability also does not mean the technology is the best fit for every weed-control problem. Suitability depends on crop, weed species, plant stage, soil condition, operating speed, safety constraints, operator training, support model, local regulation, and economics.
